## 3. Critical Weaknesses & Epistemic Limitations Identified

While the decoupled architecture successfully prevented catastrophic capital loss (exiting `ELECON` and `SHAKTIPUMP`) and captured major multi-baggers (`LUMAXTECH`, `SJS`, `CCL`), the longitudinal audit exposed **four distinct investment-decision weaknesses**:

### ⚠️ 1. Overvaluation Discipline Gap (Better at "Broken" than "Demanding")
The engine excels at detecting when a business is broken (CFO collapse, guidance retraction), but is less mature at enforcing trims when an excellent business reaches demanding valuations.
- *Example*: In `JYOTICNC` at Q4 FY25 ($73.8\times$ P/E) and `GRAVITA` at Q4 FY25 ($43.7\times$ P/E), the engine chose `CORE_HOLD`. While holding was defensible because the underlying business was healthy, both subsequently suffered material multiple compressions. The boundary between *"expensive but justified"* and *"expensive enough that expected return is unviable"* remains an active observation zone.

### ⚠️ 2. Premature Accumulation on Falling Stocks ("Catching Falling Knives")
When an underwritten thesis remains intact and P/E derates, the engine can be too eager to signal `ACCUMULATE_CONVICTION` before market expectation clearing has concluded.
- *Example*: In `JYOTICNC` (from $51\times$ to $29.8\times$), the engine signaled ADD at each step while the stock fell $-39.4\%$ to ₹669.95. The engine confused *"fundamentally becoming cheaper"* with *"capital should immediately be added."*

### ⚠️ 3. Conflating Fundamental Health with Capital Allocation Optimality
In several `Acceptable` rows (e.g., `ANANTRAJ` in FY26), decisions were graded acceptable because the data center thesis was compounding, despite negative interim 1Q/4Q returns. Fundamental health is a necessary prerequisite, but it does not automatically make `ADD` the optimal allocation choice at that price.

### ⚠️ 4. Policy Constraints vs Empirical Optimality
The declared portfolio rules (e.g., 150–250 bps per quarter, 10% max allocation, 25% trim) represent **human-defined policy risk constraints**, not mathematically optimized parameters proven by the backtest. They must be treated as portfolio governance guardrails.

## 4. Six Mandatory Deep-Dive Case Studies

### 🔬 Case Study 1: `LUMAXTECH` — The Complete ADD ➔ HOLD Rerating Lifecycle
- **Trajectory**: ₹520.70 (18.5x) ➔ ₹1,430.40 (40.3x) ➔ **₹2,049.00** (48.8x)
- **ADD Phase (Q1 FY25 - Q1 FY26)**: P/E ranged 17.2x - 29.6x. Underwritten 20% CAGR + ₹120 Cr debt reduction delivered 4Q forward return of **+92.8%** and 4Q EPS growth of **+21.0%**.
- **HOLD Phase (Q2 FY26 - Q1 FY27)**: At Q2 FY26 (₹1,430.40, 40.3x P/E), Clock 2 triggered `FULL` valuation. The system ceased new capital accumulation (`CORE_HOLD`), protecting against multiple expansion risk while letting existing position compound to ₹2,049.00 (+43.2% gain without additional capital risk).
- **Why HOLD vs TRIM at 48.8x**: Underwriting rule specifies trim trigger at documented company-specific ceiling (P/E > 50x) or Expectation Gap < 0%. At Q1 FY27, Owner's EPS expanded to ₹42.0 (operating leverage), keeping Expectation Gap positive (+3.7%) and P/E at 48.8x (just under 50x ceiling).

### 🔬 Case Study 2: `SJS` — Dissecting the Q2 FY26 ➔ Q3 FY26 Valuation Sensitivity
- **Audit of the Questioned Transition**:
  - **Q2 FY26 (Nov 2025)**: Price ₹1,758.70, EPS ₹41.00 $\implies$ P/E = **42.9x**. Implied growth = $42.9 / 3.0 = 14.3\%$. Expectation Gap = $22.0\% - 14.3\% = \mathbf{+7.7\%}$. Because $7.7\% < 8.0\%$ (`FULL_EGAP_MAX`), valuation crossed into **`FULL`** $\implies$ **`CORE_HOLD`**.
  - **Q3 FY26 (Feb 2026)**: Price ₹1,803.60, EPS expanded to ₹43.00 $\implies$ P/E compressed to **41.9x**. Implied growth = $41.9 / 3.0 = 13.97\%$. Expectation Gap = $22.0\% - 13.97\% = \mathbf{+8.03\%}$. Because $+8.03\% \ge 8.0\%$, the metric crossed the boundary back into **`REASONABLE`** $\implies$ **`ACCUMULATE_CONVICTION`**.
- **Conclusion**: The transition was mathematically legitimate and driven by **quarterly EPS expansion (+4.9% QoQ) outpacing share price growth (+2.5% QoQ)**, which derated the multiple from 42.9x to 41.9x and restored the expectation cushion.

### 🔬 Case Study 3: `JYOTICNC` — The Full TRIM ➔ HOLD ➔ RE-ENTRY Cycle
- **Trajectory**: ₹1,106.05 (88.5x) ➔ ₹1,114.70 (80.8x) ➔ ₹918.00 (51.0x) ➔ ₹669.95 (29.8x) ➔ **₹857.95** (35.7x)
- **TRIM Phase (Q1 FY25 - Q2 FY25)**: Stock traded at bubble multiples (88.5x & 80.8x P/E, E-gap +0.5%). System triggered **`PRUDENT_TRIM`**, protecting capital against subsequent **-39.4% peak drawdown** down to ₹669.95.
- **HOLD Phase (Q3 FY25 - Q4 FY25)**: Multiples cooled into 63x-73x as 3,000-machine capacity commissioned.
- **RE-ENTRY Phase (Q1 FY26 - Q1 FY27)**: In Q1 FY26, price normalized to ₹918.00 (51.0x P/E) while EPS expanded from ₹12.50 to ₹18.00 (+44%). System upgraded action to **`ACCUMULATE_CONVICTION`**, successfully capturing the bottom accumulation from ₹669.95 to ₹857.95.

### 🔬 Case Study 4: `HBLENGINE` — The Earnings-Led Multiple Derating
- **Trajectory**: Price ₹634.40 ➔ ₹674.60 (+6.3% price gain), but EPS ₹15.00 ➔ **₹42.00 (+180% EPS growth)**.
- **P/E Compression**: Multiple collapsed from **42.3x (FULL)** down to **16.1x (ATTRACTIVE)**.
- **Decision Precision**: At Q1 FY25, system held (`CORE_HOLD`). When price dipped to ₹540 in Q2 FY25 (30.9x P/E), system signaled **`ACCUMULATE_CONVICTION`** and maintained accumulation through 7 consecutive quarters as Kavach and defense contracts delivered massive earnings leverage.

### 🔬 Case Study 5: `ELECON` — Structural Breakdown & Immediate Exit
- **Initial State (Q1 FY25)**: Domestic gears healthy, European subsidiary grew +2% (straining).
- **Breakdown (Q2 FY25)**: European subsidiary collapsed to **-28.0% YoY** with guidance retracted. Causal bucket `DEMAND` breached break threshold (-15%). System immediately declared **`THESIS FALSIFIED / BROKEN`** and executed **`SYSTEMATIC_EXIT`** at **₹558.50**.
- **Downside Protection**: Stock continued to bleed down to **₹438.25 (-21.5% subsequent loss avoided)**.

### 🔬 Case Study 6: `SHAKTIPUMP` — Balance Sheet & Cash Traps Falsification
- **Immediate Falsification (Q1 FY25)**: Despite robust headline revenue growth (+40%), statutory cash flow conversion collapsed to **CFO/PAT = 0.15** with receivables ballooning to **165 days** and debt expanding.
- **Action**: Causal bucket `CASH` failed immediately. System executed **`SYSTEMATIC_EXIT`** at **₹745.85** on Day 1.
- **Capital Preserved**: Prevented a catastrophic **-32.5% loss** down to **₹503.20** as auditor qualifications and receivable write-downs materialized.

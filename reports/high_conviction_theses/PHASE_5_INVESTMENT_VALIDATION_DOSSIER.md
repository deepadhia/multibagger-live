## 3. Hostile Assumption Stress Tests (Deep-Dive)

### Case 1: Testing `LUMAXTECH` (#1 Dislocation)
- **Market Question**: *"Why was the market pricing only 2.9% growth at 18.5x P/E?"*
- **Empirical Answer**: The market perceived Lumax as a low-margin legacy lighting manufacturer tied to 2-wheeler OEM cycles.
- **Underwriting Defense**: The acquisition and operational integration of IAC India transformed product mix into higher-margin interior cockpits for high-growth SUV platforms (Mahindra, Tata). EBITDA margins expanded +140 bps, ROCE rose to 22.5%, and consolidated PAT grew +36.5% with cash conversion of 0.84.
- **Asymmetric Payoff**: Even under a harsh **Bear case of 15% CAGR**, the market expectation gap was $+12.1\%$. The subsequent +184.2% real-world return verified this intrinsic dislocation.

### Case 2: Testing `ANANTRAJ` (#3 Dislocation)
- **Market Question**: *"Can we defend a 35% growth trajectory?"*
- **Hostile Challenge**: No. 35% CAGR requires flawless simultaneous execution of 300 MW data center phases without power delay or pricing pressure from hyperscalers.
- **Epistemic Adjustment**: We downgrade the base projection from 35% to **22.0% CAGR**.
- **Result**: At 24.5x P/E, 22.0% Base growth still produces an expectation asymmetry of $+14.8\%$ against the market-implied 7.2%, but eliminates the fragility of a single heroic estimate.

### Case 3: Testing `JYOTICNC` (#11 Trim Candidate)
- **Market Question**: *"The business delivered +40% revenue growth and 22.5% ROCE with clean cash — why not buy?"*
- **Underwriting Defense**: Price detached from fundamentals. At 113.3x P/E, the market implied growth rate was **65.5% CAGR**. Even if the business delivers its maximum Bull case (30% CAGR), it falls 35.5% short of what the multiple requires.
- **Capital Action**: `PRUDENT_TRIM`. The ranking layer properly separates business quality from investment upside.

## 4. Operational Guidelines for Phase 6 & 7

1. **Zero Heuristic Drift**: No new scoring algorithms or rule expansions will be added to the codebase.
2. **Explicit "I Don't Know" Policy**: If management commentary is ambiguous, statutory filings are incomplete, or capacity timelines are untracked, the equity is marked `MISPRICING CLAIM: UNVERIFIED` and paused from capital additions.
3. **Continuous Decision Journaling**: Every quarter, actual XBRL filings are ingested, blind assumption inference runs, and decisions (`ACCUMULATE`, `HOLD`, `PAUSE`, `TRIM`, `EXIT`) are committed to the point-in-time audit ledger for multi-year tracking.
